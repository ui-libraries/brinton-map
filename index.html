Brinton Show Map

Brinton show dates, venues, and receipts, 1904–1908. Use the date slider and filters to choose which shows appear. The same shows are listed in the data table below the map.

Date and filters

Arrow keys move one day. Page Up and Page Down move about 10 percent of the range. Home and End jump to the first and last dates.

Map of show locations

Interactive map. Each show is a button on the map. Press Tab to move between show markers, then Enter or Space to open its details, and Escape to close them. Arrow keys pan the map, plus and minus zoom. A data table with the same information follows the map.

Data table of shows

This table lists the shows currently visible on the map, in date order. It updates as you change the date slider or filters.
